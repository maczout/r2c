## Does Response accept each edited file

- **Solved**: r2x solved every one of the 53 (the captures).
- **Read as given**: Response's own Save As of each variant has every edited attribute
  (`docs/scope-probes.md`, column "Save As"; the differences there are formatting, the
  values Response derives for a segmental steel curve, and the zero it adds for the other
  bar layer in `<rebarthermx>`).
- **The input screen**: r2x's report of each variant was made and kept outside the
  repository. Looked at by eye: the moment axis ("Axial load applied 200 mm from bottom"),
  the bar's thermal strain ("Variable Concrete Shrinkage/Thermal Strains"), interlocking
  hoops (the hoop is drawn differently) and one softening model. **The input screen does
  not show a concrete's models** (base curve number, softening, tension stiffening): for
  those 26 cases the Save As is the evidence that Response read the edit, and the change
  in its results that it used it. Reading, for the owner to confirm, that this meets "the
  input screen must show the edit" where the screen has no place for it. The other
  screens were not looked at one by one.

## Tried and dropped

- **Reading the binary for the drape's rule** through the XML reader of a layer
  (`00519540`, `0051c8b0`) and the old text reader (`00488e60`, which stores DRAPE at
  offset 0x10 of a layer record of 0xb6 bytes): the analysis does not address the field in
  a form that a search for the offset finds. Stopped after four steps; see open questions.
  The thesis shows results for draped strands (10-10) and gives no rule.

## Decisions

- Owner (2026-11-21, the brief): the list of cases "at least"; one feature per case; the
  inputs by script; fixtures in their own commit; the baseline before any kernel change.
- Developer: both groups captured, A and B (see the log of step 1).
- Developer: T-beam with shear at M/V = 2 m (the section is 1200 mm deep); softening 9 and
  11 at M/V = 1 m with the I-girder at M/V = 1 m as their base (`igirder-mv`).
- Developer: "factors from the file" and "units from the file" are compared with the
  options given, as every case is: a fixture's header records the settings in effect, not
  whether an option set them, and `tools/verify.py` passes them all. That r2c takes them
  from the file when no option is given is a matter for a test of the reader (step 3).
- Developer: the fixtures of the scope cases are cut at the feature stages like the
  others; 9.6 MB in all. Why: the views at the features are what the targets compare.

## Open questions for the owner

1. The input screen and the concrete models (above).
2. **Draped tendons**: neither the thesis nor a few steps in the binary gave the rule.
   Measured: a drape of 0.05 on eight of twelve strands changes Response's peak by less
   than 0.1 % and every chart a little; it is not the vertical component of the prestress
   (that would be about 50 kN of some 600). r2c ignores the drape and is inside on peak and
   rising branch on the case. Should the rule be searched for further in the binary, or
   inferred from Response's output, or left with the drape ignored and recorded as a known
   deviation?
