## What the measurements showed (against the brief's expectations)

- **Curved outline rows cannot be in an input file.** Response's XML reader rejects a lone
  UP or DOWN line and a width of UP or DOWN alike ("Error in Quotes!"). In the binary the
  two words are used by the reader of shape.dat (`004f96e0`) and by the width field of the
  outline dialog (`00448d90`); a circle defined in Response is saved as 13 straight rows.
  Nothing to build; option C4 falls away.
- **A shape without an outline is not solved by Response**: the file opens with no concrete
  and the solve fails ("bad request"). The brief lists it as a refusal to build; r2c already
  accepts it; there is nothing to compare it with.
- **T-headed stirrups are compared already**: Newbeam's own stirrup is the T-headed single
  leg (pattern 5). On Newbeam, patterns 1, 2, 3, 5 and 6 give the same charts byte for byte;
  only the hoop (4) and, on the circular column, interlocking hoops (6) differ.
- **Compression softening hardly shows on Newbeam** (under 0.3 % on the peak at M/V = 1 m);
  on the I-girder at M/V = 2 m the models move the peak by −9 to +6 %. The softening cases
  are on the I-girder.
- **Tension stiffening "None" cannot be captured**: Response asks a question before solving
  and r2x stops at it. Tension stiffening 5 is refused by Response.
- **A segmental curve for prestressing steel is dropped by Response on reading**; one for
  reinforcing steel is read and used (r2c reads and ignores it).
- **Draped tendons change every chart but the peak by less than 0.1 %** on the I-girder
  (r2c ignores the drape).
- r2c reads and ignores a shrinkage profile (`<shrinktherm>`); Response applies it.
- r2c's formulas for softening models 2, 6, 7, 10, 13 have no source in `docs/kernel.md`;
  they are "not compared" until their cases say otherwise.

## Tried and dropped

- **Softening cases on Newbeam.** Probed for all 18 numbers; the feature does not show
  there. Kept as probes (rows newbeam-csoftN of the generated page), not as cases.
- **Softening models 9 and 11 on the I-girder at M/V = 2 m**: r2x's own check stops both
  ("Response has more than the N stages on its control curves", twice each). At M/V = 1 m
  it captures them; the I-girder at M/V = 1 m without the feature is added as their base.
- **Three load combinations on Newbeam** (constant tension with M + V; a constant moment
  with shear alone; axial load and shear together): r2x or Response fails on each. Not
  tried on other sections.
- **Getting a curved row past Response's reader** by another spelling: not tried further
  once the binary showed where the words are used.
