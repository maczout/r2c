# 2026-11-21-01: acceptance targets, and the tools to measure them

Who: the developer (a coding agent), with the owner, interactively.
Branch at the end: `acceptance-tooling` (from `shear-iteration`; pull request to `main`).

## Goal

The owner set acceptance targets for current development and asked for the measurements to
be completed in the tools before the targets were committed. This was meta-development (the
owner's term, 2026-11-21): the tests, tooling and criteria that development is measured
against, done with the owner deciding each proposal as it came up. No kernel change.

## Done

- `docs/acceptance.md`: the owner's targets (below), committed once the tools measured them.
- `tools/compare.py`: rising and post-peak measured both ways, split at Response's peak; the
  curve's shape (features in order); detail views at each feature stage of Response's curve,
  r2c's stage matched by load and control value together; the printed-precision floor.
- `tools/report.py`, `tools/dashboard.py`: the new checks; case labels in engineering terms
  beside the IDs; on the page, one marker per check in place of one verdict.
- `tools/retrim.py`: re-cuts the fixtures from the archive to hold the feature stages. Run by
  the owner in r2dev, with the verify rerun, since the archive and Response's sample inputs
  are there.
- `docs/verification.md`: a section on the results against the targets. `docs/status.md`
  brought up to date.

## Tried and dropped

- **"Yield" as a shape feature** (a second drop in stiffness): on the 25 cases it also found
  other softening (yield in r2c's Liping LB1 but not Response's; Marshall's at 30 against
  13 rad/km). Differences of the detector, not of the programs. Dropped.
- **A precision explanation for the detail-view misses.** All 85 view comparisons above 5 % at
  the peak were checked against Response's printed precision: none lies within two units of
  the last digit. The floor is kept as a guard; it changes no result. Not to be looked for
  again.

## Decisions

All the owner's, 2026-11-21, unless marked.

- Priority: clone Response's behaviour naively (software). No structural assessment of
  results will be given in this project. Where r2c and Response differ, Response is right.
- All branches of the curves matter, post-peak included, and every detail view.
- Axial and moment cases: match qualitatively (curve shape) and numerically within target.
  Shear cases: the same aim, harder; minor deviations may be accepted depending on progress.
- Targets for development: ±5 % on every metric, absolute limits for small values near the
  numerical precision; reviewed when the focus moves from development to testing. The
  present 25 cases are right for proof of concept; range reviewed when the scope is complete.
- The owner's read of earlier sessions: the developer was chasing an unreasonably tight
  match, which distracted from building out the scope.
- Accepted on the developer's proposals: the two-way rising/post-peak measure; the floor as
  two units of the last printed digit, read from the data; the shape features (cracking,
  falls of load, peak, how the response ends; yield dropped); views matched by load and
  control value together, at the feature stages; one marker per check on the page.
- Developer, reading for the owner to confirm: Response's last stage in Newbeam M/V = 0.5 m
  (a drop to 4.6 kN at nearly zero curvature) is behaviour to match, not an artefact to filter.
- Developer, reading for the owner to confirm: the stance puts options A3 and G (Response's
  stage acceptance and end-of-response rules) in scope for development, where they had
  waited on the owner's word.
- Developer: shape thresholds (stiffness below 0.4 × the first step's for cracking; a fall is
  2 % of Response's peak) fitted on the 25 cases; recorded in `tools/compare.py`.
