  stage in any of the twelve shear cases; its responses end where no state is found.
  Response's collapsed last stage comes from its own acceptance of a stage (A3, out of

## Decisions

- Owner (2026-11-21, the brief): scope first, then tighten; the seven cases, "Newbeam with
  tendons added" dropped; for this increment the regression rule is that peak, rising
  branch and shape of the 25 cases must not go outside, and a view check may change sides
  only with an explanation; the thesis is kept at `~/references/bentz-thesis/`.
- Developer: curved outline segments not built. Why: the brief asks for them "if the column
  needs them" and it does not; there is no oracle case to check them against.
- Developer: the two sides of a step in width are kept apart in the profile and in the
  stirrup ratio only; the depth-wise tables are still interpolated across a step. Why: the
  first two were what stopped the I-girder; the tables are views' business here.
- Developer: the last-stage shape difference of the two shear cases is recorded as a known
  deviation and not worked on. Why: A3 is out of scope in the brief.
- Developer: the Quick Define inputs are not copied into r2c; tests find them through
  `R2C_DEFINE_DIR`, verify through `--inputs`. Why: CLAUDE.md keeps inputs out of the
  repository, and the brief puts their folder in the capture recipe.
- Developer: `tools/capture.py --add` and `tools/verify.py`'s input search were changed in
  their own commit, the fixtures added in another, both before the baseline. They are
  listed under "Measurement changes" in the pull request.
