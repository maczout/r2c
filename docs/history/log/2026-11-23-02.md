## Tried and dropped

- **Blending two views point by point.** Not possible in general: Response layers the
  section afresh at each stage, so in about three views of four the neighbours' lines
  have different points (63, 79 and 93 points for the longitudinal strain at three
  successive stages of M/V = 1 m). Point by point is kept only where the points are the
  same in depth (the steel charts, drawn as boxes, which cannot be blended by depth).
- **Keeping the neighbours as ordinary stages of the fixture** (`stage/N/VIEW`): dropped
  before it was written. Everything that reads a fixture takes its stages to be the
  stages to compare, so the comparison, the regression tests and the tools would each
  have needed telling. Under a name of their own nothing else sees them.
- **Looking for why Response gives 15 or 25 stages on the I-girder with shear** was not
  started: it is not this session's work, and the Wine prefix was ruled out in one run
  only. What is known is in `docs/status.md`.
- **A check at a view that cannot be blended.** Left assessed, not called unstable: the
  rule says a check is left unassessed by no other route. It happens twice on the 29
  (shrinkage and thermal strain at the peak of the two −M cases; r2c's difference there
  is nothing).

## Decisions

- Owner, 2026-11-23 (recorded on `main` before the session): D54, the rule and that the
  developer carries it out as a change to the measure.
- Developer (reading, put to the owner as D55): the stage's place between its neighbours
  is by distance along the control curve, scaled as the curves are measured. Why: it is
  defined on every branch; by load alone or control value alone the stage is not between
  its neighbours at a peak or a fall of load.
- Developer (reading, D55): views are blended by depth, a step in a line kept as a step;
  a line beyond its ends keeps its end value (no view on the 29 needs that).
- Developer (reading, D55): a line one neighbour does not draw is blended with nothing.
  The other reading changes no check.
- Developer: the control curve used is the bottom control chart, the one the stages are
  matched on (D13, D42); the limit is `views_pct` and the floor the comparison's own, two
  units of the last printed digit, as the rule states.
- Developer: "as small as the rule needs" read as: only the 29 cases; nothing for a
  compared stage that is first or last (not tested); a neighbour that is itself compared
  kept once. The parked fixtures were not touched; on them nothing is tested.
- Developer: the one-way measure is kept as the comparison has it (Response's points
  from the other curve), so a line both neighbours draw and the stage does not is not
  counted. It does not occur on the 29.
- Developer: the rule's reach (one check in five, most of them inside) is reported and
  put to the owner as D56, not acted on. Whether a match at a jump is luck is structural.
- Developer: the fixture of the I-girder with shear was not replaced by today's 25-stage
  capture. Why: a fixture is not changed without the owner; the old capture is a real
  one (it came again once today); nothing compared differs between the two. Put to the
  owner as D57.
- Developer: `src/r2c` was not touched; the helper that reads a neighbour's view is in
  the tools.
