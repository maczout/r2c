## Goal

Meta-development: carry out the owner's answer to D56. (1) Change the test in the measure
to the spike test as `docs/acceptance.md` states it: unstable only where the two
neighbours agree within `views_pct` and the stage departs from their blend by more than
`views_pct`; no new captures. (2) Rerun verify and report; give for each tier the checks
inside, outside and unassessed under no rule, the rule as first written and the spike
test; list every check left unassessed, Musts first; show the bond chart at the end of
the prestrained stirrups as a test of the rule. (3) If it catches almost nothing, say so.
No kernel change.

## Tried and dropped

- **Measuring the neighbours on the scale of the chart at the stage** as the reading in
  use. Written first, because it puts both conditions in the same units and would let
  "0.1, 38, 0.2 MPa" count as agreeing neighbours. Dropped for the plain comparison
  (each neighbour on its own largest value, both ways): that is what the wording says,
  and on the 29 cases the two give the same four checks. Kept as the other reading in
  `tools/unstable.py --readings`.
- **Making the bond chart come out caught.** Not attempted. It is not a spike by the test
  under either reading (neighbours 71 % apart at the peak on their own scale, 111 % on
  the stage's), and changing the test until it is would be fitting the measure to a
  result. Reported as it is (D59).
- **Changing the one-way distance measure** so that a drop to nothing shows as a
  departure (stage 19 of the bond chart, 4.9 %). Not attempted: it is the comparison's
  measure, used by every view check, and outside this session's brief. Noted in
  `docs/status.md` and under D59's evidence in the summary.

## Decisions

- Owner, 2026-11-23 (recorded on `main` before the session): D56, the spike test and its
  wording; D55, the three readings confirmed; D57.
- Developer (reading, put to the owner as D58): "agree with each other" is the
  comparison's measure taken both ways, each neighbour on its own largest value, the
  larger of the two, with the printed-precision floor of each. Why: neither neighbour is
  the reference, and nothing is added to the comparison's measure.
- Developer: "within `views_pct`" includes the target itself (at most 5 %), and "more
  than `views_pct`" excludes it, as the comparison counts a difference at the target
  inside.
- Developer: a result that carries a departure but no neighbours' distance (a verify run
  made before this change) is not tested, so stays assessed; a check is left unassessed
  by no other route.
- Developer: the rule as first written is kept in the code only for counting beside the
  spike test (`is_unstable(..., "departure")`); nothing is assessed by it.
